# Privacy Policy — Glory of Rome

_Last updated: September 21, 2026_

Glory of Rome ("the app") is a single-player strategy game. This policy explains
what the app does with your information: **nothing.**

## Data we collect

**None.** The app does not collect, store, transmit or share any personal
information or usage data. Specifically:

- It requests **no Android permissions** and holds **no internet permission** —
  it cannot send anything off your device.
- There are **no accounts, logins or sign-ins.**
- There is **no analytics, telemetry, advertising or third-party SDK** of any
  kind.
- Your saved games and settings stay in the app's own storage on your device
  and are never transmitted.

Because the app cannot reach the network, no data ever leaves your device.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages. Since it collects no data from anyone, it
collects no data from children.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party services, ad networks or trackers, and shares
data with no one — there is no data to share.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes its saved games along with it. There is nothing
held anywhere else to delete.
